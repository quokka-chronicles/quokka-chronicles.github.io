# Two Sides of the Scar

The descent was not an empty plunge, but a crushing immersion.

Kevin couldn’t move. At first, he didn’t even understand why.

Kevin’s legs were bound fast in a thick, suffocating sludge that reeked of ozone and ancient stone. Every attempt to pull his paws free felt like dragging them through cooling tar. He thrashed, his muscles burning as he scraped the viscous residue from his face, forcing his golden eyes open against the oppressive density.

The world was upside down—or perhaps sideways. Above him, the air thinned into a pale, violet mist. And there, suspended in the unnatural stillness, was {{qk.characterName}}. Her small form drifted overhead, her crimson back-line pulsing like a dying ember.

As he fought his way upward into the lighter air, the strange, backwards flow of time took hold. His jaw moved before his thoughts could catch up, uttering words he felt he had already heard.

"...{{qk.characterName}}—" he rasped, his voice echoing from a place behind his own ears.

"Kevin—!" her sudden cry was filled with a desperate, breathless relief that pierced through the haze.

Determined to bridge the impossible gap, he stretched his limbs against the resistance of the heavy air. "Wait, I’ll catch you."

"Come on, reach out your paw," she pleaded, her eyes wide as she tried to paddle through the empty space toward him.

Kevin paused, a cold shiver running along his spine as her voice vibrated in his mind before her mouth even formed the shapes. "Hm... I heard that before you even said it."

He clawed frantically through the thinning slime, watching her face twist in bewilderment. Her voice reached his ears like an echo rebounding off a wall that hadn't been built yet.

"Kevin… please…" she whimpered, her ears pinning back in confusion. "What are you saying?"

"...the same thing as you," he answered, his white fur flickering like an unstable shadow "Just backward."

{{qk.characterName}} tilted her head, searching his pale face for answers in a world that offered none. "Then what do you want us to do?"

"...if we get any closer, we might open the scar again," he warned, his tone heavy with a dread he hadn't yet fully processed "That rift from which there is no return."

With every syllable that left his throat, the heavy fluid around Kevin dissipated further. The air grew lighter, hollower, almost weightless, making his skin prickle with static electricity.

"What are you talking about? Just a little closer…" {{qk.characterName}} urged, ignoring the warning as her red stripe flared brighter in response to his presence "...and we’ll touch. Please, help me!"

"...don’t worry, I’m almost there," Kevin whispered, his amber eyes fixed on her form. "Just differently than you."

Tears welling in her eyes, {{qk.characterName}} looked at him with an intensity that made his chest ache. "Kevin… I don’t want you to fall into the darkness again…"

"But I feel like I’m still falling down," he admitted, his paws numbly reaching for an anchor that wasn't there.

"Look around!" she cried out, waving her tiny paws at the endless, shimmering expanse. "There is no down here, no direction exists. We can’t be near or far."

Kevin’s heart hammered furiously against his ribs. The atmosphere had turned so thin now that breathing felt like inhaling pure vacuum, burning his lungs with every gasp. The reality around them shuddered, bleeding into a blinding, featureless white haze.

"...that’s why," he growled softly, a mixture of fear and affection tearing him apart inside. "When we’re too close, we tear the world around us. We create this mist. I... am dangerous."

"Why?" she snapped back stubbornly, her love overriding her terror. "I’m right here. I’m here with you!"

"...I felt you before I saw you," he confessed, the memory of her touch washing over him out of order. "That shouldn’t be possible. {{qk.characterName}}... this isn’t how it’s supposed to be!"

"But we won’t miss each other completely," {{qk.characterName}} insisted, her glowing tail tracing a trail of red sparks through the violet air "I can feel you closer now."

He tried to push forward with all his strength, but his movement reversed mid-stride, folding back onto itself like an optical illusion.

"I don’t know," he panted, his throat dry as bone. "Every step I take here weirdly folds backward as soon as I make it. I guess that’s why we keep missing each other."

"I can’t move here," she sobbed softly, helpless against the invisible currents. "Tell me how to get to you. Please, try."

"The current is pushing me away, but..." Kevin observed, his vision blurs as space bent around them, "...it feels like I’m getting closer after all?"

"I’m trying," {{qk.characterName}} gasped, reaching out her small arm as far as it would stretch. "Everything here moves on its own. But I feel like it’s... carrying me to you."

Kevin watched the space between them collapse, the reality folding inwards like a crushed leaf. "...I see," he murmured softly. "It looked like we wouldn’t reach each other. Something was holding me tight."

{{qk.characterName}}’s eyes locked onto his, shining with an unfiltered, raw emotion. "Kevin… I’m finally with you. I was so scared."

Their paws brushed—an impossible, brief touch of warmth.

In that exact instant, the thinning air vanished completely, turning into a terrifying vacuum. A violent, unseen pull clamped around Kevin’s chest, snatching him backward with brutal force. The scar opened its throat, and he was yanked back down into the rushing dark.

There was no rush of wind, no plummeting acceleration. {{qk.characterName}} fell, but the abyss did not swallow her whole—it trapped her in a silent, suffocating void. 

She was floating in an empty vacuum where no air existed. Her lungs burned, starved for oxygen as her chest heaved in silent, desperate panic. She clawed at the air, trying to scream, trying to gasp, but not a single sound or breath left her muzzle. Then, below her, she saw him. 

Kevin was trapped in a dense, translucent gel that clung to his pale fur like cooling resin. He thrashed violently, scraping the sludge from his eyes and jaw, fighting his way through the heavy, liquid darkness. She reached out, mouthing his name in utter silence, begging her body to move toward him. 

Gradually, a subtle shift rippled through the void. The vacuum began to yield. Cold, sharp static tingled against her skin, and a thin, metallic vapor reached her nostrils. Her chest expanded, drinking in the first gasp of thick, violet-tinted mist. As the air around her grew denser, her voice tore through her throat, desperate and raw.

"Kevin… I’m finally with you. I was so scared."

Below her, Kevin paused mid-movement, his amber eyes locking onto hers as the gel around his limbs dissolved into thin vapor. "...I see," he murmured softly, his voice echoing from everywhere at once. "It looked like we wouldn’t reach each other. Something was holding me tight."

{{qk.characterName}} kicked her hind paws, trying to swim through the thickening atmosphere that seemed to pull her forward on invisible currents. "I’m trying," she gasped, reaching out her small arm as far as it would stretch. "Everything here moves on its own. But I feel like it’s... carrying me to you."

Kevin’s form flickered like an unstable reflection on water as he drifted upward toward her. "The current is pushing me away, but..." he observed, his vision blurring as space bent around them, "...it feels like I’m getting closer after all?"

"I can’t move here," she sobbed softly, helpless against the invisible, heavy currents dragging at her fur. "Tell me how to get to you. Please, try."

He panted, the air around him thinning while the space around {{qk.characterName}} pressed tighter against her limbs. "I don’t know. Every step I take here weirdly folds backward as soon as I make it. I guess that’s why we keep missing each other."

The crimson stripe along her back ignited, casting a brilliant bloody glow through the murky atmosphere. "But we won’t miss each other completely," {{qk.characterName}} insisted, her glowing tail tracing a trail of red sparks through the violet air. "I can feel you closer now."

Kevin’s ears pinned back, his expression a mix of awe and haunting realization. "...I felt you before I saw you," he confessed, his gaze fixed on her. "That shouldn’t be possible. {{qk.characterName}}... this isn’t how it’s supposed to be!"

"Why?" she snapped back stubbornly, her love overriding her terror as she tried to bridge the gap. "I’m right here. I’m here with you!"

He shook his head, his pale fur shivering as the mist rippled violently around them. "...that’s why. When we’re too close, we tear the world around us. We create this mist. I... am dangerous."

Tears welled in her eyes, blurring his white silhouette. "Kevin… I don’t want you to fall into the darkness again…"

"...don’t worry, I’m almost there," Kevin whispered, his amber eyes locking onto hers with tragic warmth. "Just differently than you."

The air around {{qk.characterName}} was turning heavy now, almost like cold water, resisting her every movement. "What are you talking about? Just a little closer…" she urged, ignoring the heavy pressure crushing her ribs "...and we’ll touch. Please, help me!"

"...if we get any closer, we might open the scar again," he warned, his tone heavy with a dread that echoed backward into the void. "That rift from which there is no return."

She stared at him, her mind spinning as his words seemed to answer questions she hadn't yet asked. "Then what do you want us to do?"

"...the same thing as you," he answered, his white fur flickering like an unstable shadow. "Just backward."

"Kevin… please…" she whimpered, her ears pinning back in confusion. "What are you saying?"

He paused, a strange shiver running through him as if catching an echo from a future moment. "Hm... I heard that before you even said it."

The space between them compressed into a heavy, syrupy mass. She could barely lift her arm. "Come on, reach out your paw," she pleaded, her eyes wide as she fought the heavy air.

"Wait, I’ll catch you," he called out, straining against the thinning void on his side.

"Kevin—!" her sudden cry tore from her throat, filled with a desperate, breathless relief that pierced through the haze.

"...{{qk.characterName}}—" he rasped, his voice sounding like a distant whisper behind her ears.

Their paws touched. 

An explosive surge of energy erupted at the point of contact. Instantly, Kevin was violently snatched upward—catapulted into the blinding white void above and vanishing in a flash of light.

{{qk.characterName}} was left alone, the air around her hardened rapidly, congealing into thick, invisible bands that wrapped tightly around her chest, her limbs, and her tail. It felt as though hundreds of heavy, unbreakable ropes were binding her, pinning her paws to her sides and sealing her jaws shut.

She struggled, but the pressure grew absolute, cocooning her in an impenetrable, heavy shell. Darkness crept over her eyes as the invisible weight pressed down, cutting off the light. Yet, as her senses faded into the heavy, motionless dark, her last spiraling thought echoed through her mind: Kevin…
